GLOBAL CLIMATE CONTEXT

High-Mountain Cryosphere Collapse & Climate Loss

The August 26, 2026 Rasuwa disaster occurred when a 49-acre rock-ice chunk detached from the Langtang Lirung massif at 5,200m elevation. Friction and impact converted the ice into a mobile debris torrent surging 80m above riverbed levels. High-Mountain Asia warming rates have accelerated structural permafrost degradation, converting seasonal ice reservoirs into violent cascades. Nepal has formally submitted a $20 Million claim under the UN Climate Loss and Damage Framework.
